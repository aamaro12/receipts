# Demo questions and sources

## Finance (`demo/finance`)

**Question:** "What were each company's 2024 operating margin and 2022-2024 revenue CAGR? Use net sales as Diageo's revenue; Diageo's fiscal year ends on 30 June."

The question avoids "EBITDA margin" and "3-year CAGR". The documents can't support either: in an earlier run, an operating profit relabelled as EBITDA and a cube root taken over two years both passed the check.

| File | Company and report | Source | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| `relx_income_statement.pdf` | RELX, 2024 Annual Report: consolidated income statement and statement of comprehensive income | https://www.relx.com/~/media/Files/R/RELX-Group/documents/reports/annual-reports/relx-2024-annual-report.pdf | PDF pages 142-143 (0-based 141-142); printed pages 140-141 |
| `diageo_income_statement.pdf` | Diageo, Financial statements 2024 (fiscal year ended 30 June 2024): consolidated income statement, statement of comprehensive income, balance sheet | https://www.diageo.com/~/media/Files/D/Diageo-V2/Diageo-Corp/investors/results-reports-and-events/annual-reports/financial-statements-2024.pdf | PDF pages 10-12; printed pages 161-163 |

- **RELX** reports in GBP million. Basic earnings per share are in pence.
- **Diageo** reports in USD million. Basic earnings per share are in US cents.

## Logistics (`demo/logistics`)

**Question:** "Can we release container MSKU4417302? Compare gross weight and declared value across the bill of lading, commercial invoice and arrival notice."

The logistics documents are synthetic, generated by `make_logistics_docs.py`. There are three:
- **Bill of lading:** gross weight 18,420 kg.
- **Commercial invoice:** declared value USD 759,500.00.
- **Arrival notice:** verified gross weight 19,240 kg, and a rule that release is withheld when the verified weight differs from the bill of lading by more than 2%.

The expected answer is a hold. The difference is 820 kg (4.45%), computed from inputs cited on two documents.
